# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains the core process guidance for OctoAcme projects. These documents provide a lightweight, repeatable framework for planning, delivery, communication, release, and continuous improvement across cross-functional work.

## Project management process summary

OctoAcme uses a simple, cross-functional project management model that starts with initiation and stakeholder alignment, moves into planning and backlog definition, tracks progress through execution and team communication, manages risk and dependencies, and closes with release and retrospective activities.

The model is designed to keep projects transparent, iterative, and focused on business value:

- Initiation: validate the problem, align stakeholders, define goals, and decide whether to proceed.
- Planning: turn the approved initiative into a backlog, milestones, dependencies, and delivery expectations.
- Execution: deliver in small increments, track progress, manage blockers, and maintain consistent reporting.
- Risk & communication: identify issues early, escalate effectively, and keep stakeholders informed.
- Release & deployment: validate readiness, deploy carefully, and verify outcomes.
- Retrospective & improvement: capture lessons learned and convert them into clear action items.

## How these docs fit together

Use these resources together as a project lifecycle:

1. Start with the [Project Management Overview](./octoacme-project-management-overview.md) and [Project Initiation Guide](./octoacme-project-initiation.md) to confirm the need, stakeholders, and goals.
2. Move into [Project Planning](./octoacme-project-planning.md) to define scope, backlog, milestones, and the Definition of Done.
3. Use [Execution & Tracking](./octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) to manage delivery cadence, status, and blockers.
4. Apply [Risk Management & Communication](./octoacme-risks-and-communication.md) for risk review, escalation, and stakeholder updates.
5. Prepare releases with the [Release & Deployment Guide](./octoacme-release-and-deployment.md).
6. Close the loop with the [Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](./octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md) document.

## Intended use

These docs are meant to support a consistent, transparent, and collaborative project practice. They help teams share context, reduce single-point dependency risk, and keep work aligned to customer and business value.

If you are starting a new project, begin with initiation. If you are in active delivery, use the execution, risk, and release docs as your operating guide.
